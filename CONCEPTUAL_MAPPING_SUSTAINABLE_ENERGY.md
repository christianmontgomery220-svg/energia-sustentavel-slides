# Conceptual Mapping: Sustainable Energy Module

**Author:** Manus AI
**Date:** March 05, 2026

## 1. Overview and Purpose

The **Sustainable Energy** module will be a new, **independent and self-contained**, high-level module within Anonymo AI. It will be positioned in the **Educational** category, but will function as a separate experience, with its own scope and boundaries. Its purpose is to offer a space for learning and reflection on the energy transition, connecting technical data with the psychological and existential dimensions of the theme.

The module is not intended to convince the user of a specific view, but rather to provide a structured map of information so that they can form their own understanding, observing their own patterns of reaction to the content.

## 2. Module Structure

The module will follow the Anonymo interaction pattern, with an activation screen and a submodule structure. Navigation will be free, allowing the user to explore the themes in any order.

### 2.1. Activation Screen

- **Title:** Sustainable Energy Module
- **Description:** "A space to explore the data and narratives about the future of energy. To begin, activate the module."
- **Button:** "Activate"

### 2.2. Submodules

| # | Submodule | Main Content | TFB/TFC Connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Transition in Data** | Presentation of the 12 slides on the future of sustainable energy. | Capture of interest patterns (which slides are most viewed, in what order). |
| 2 | **The Personal Impact** | Reflective questions about how the energy transition affects the user's life (cost of living, career, etc.). | Capture of patterns of anxiety, hope, or skepticism. |
| 3 | **Narratives of the Future** | Exploration of different future scenarios (optimistic, pessimistic, realistic) based on the data. | Capture of patterns of affinity with certain narratives. |
| 4 | **The Dilemma of Action** | Reflection on the individual and collective role in the energy transition, without prescribing actions. | Capture of patterns of feelings of agency or powerlessness. |

## 3. Integration with TFB/TFC

The capture of longitudinal patterns will be the pillar of integration with TFB/TFC. The system will record:

- **Engagement:** Which submodules are most accessed and for how long.
- **Reactivity:** Which themes generate the most responses or interactions.
- **Sentiment:** Sentiment analysis of open-ended responses to identify emotional patterns.
- **Connections:** How the patterns observed in this module connect with patterns from other modules (e.g., Mental Health, Blind Spot).

## 4. Ethical and Security Principles

| Principle | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Neutrality** | The content will be presented in a factual and balanced manner, without taking sides in political or ideological debates. |
| **Privacy** | All interaction data will be anonymized and linked to the user's chip, without personal identification. |
| **Non-Prescription** | The module will not suggest actions, investments, or behavioral changes. The focus is on reflection and user autonomy. |
| **Easy Exit** | The user can exit the module at any time, and the system will not insist or try to bring them back. |
