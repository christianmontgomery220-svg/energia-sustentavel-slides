## Slide Structure

1. **Slide 1 – Title**: Introduction to the accelerated energy transition
2. **Slide 2 – The Energy Inflection Point**: Current context with 30% renewables
3. **Slide 3 – The Solar Revolution at Scale**: 300% growth expected by 2030
4. **Slide 4 – Battery Storage**: Battery technologies and global capacity
5. **Slide 5 – Electrification of Transport & Sectors**: Growing demand for clean electricity
6. **Slide 6 – Green Hydrogen**: Fuel for heavy industry and aviation
7. **Slide 7 – AI-Powered Smart Grids**: Real-time optimisation
8. **Slide 8 – The Infrastructure Bottleneck**: Transmission challenges and investment needs
9. **Slide 9 – Socioeconomic Impacts**: Job creation and just transition
10. **Slide 10 – Investment & Financing**: Capital flows and financial instruments
11. **Slide 11 – Vision 2030**: A scenario for a clean-energy world
12. **Slide 12 – Q&A & Contact**: Closing and open dialogue

## How to Use

### For Presentations

1. Open the slides in a browser or presentation software
2. Refer to `slide_notes.md` for the full script
3. Each note provides context, key data, and transitions between slides
4. Estimated duration: 20–25 minutes for the complete presentation

### For Self-Study

1. Read the slides in the provided sequence
2. Consult the script for deeper exploration of each topic
3. Use the images as visual references for the concepts
4. Review key statistics and data for retention

### For Printing

1. Export the slides to PDF if required
2. The `slide_notes.md` file can be printed as supporting material
3. WebP images can be converted to PNG/JPG as needed
